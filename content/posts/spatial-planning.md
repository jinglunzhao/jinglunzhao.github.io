We distinguish precise execution from the harder problem of finding a route: how should an agent use limited space, sequence movements, and improve a feasible solution?

Using parking as a testbed, we progressively tighten constraints and add obstacles, then evaluate feasibility, route quality, and consistency across repeated runs. The article includes side-by-side replays for six parking cases, a model's saved candidate routes, and 180 results from five repeats per model and case. A 3D sofa-carrying example explores the next step beyond planar motion.
